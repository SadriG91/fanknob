fanknob Help GitHub

Your Mac's fans, on a knob.

Your Mac decides how loud it wants to be. This lets you decide instead — turn the fans down when you want quiet, or up when you're pushing it hard. Set a speed and it stays there, or pick a curve and it'll follow the heat on its own.

The app

The fan goes blue whenever you're the one in charge.

What it does

One knob, every fan

Fans don't all spin at the same speeds, so you get 0–100 instead of raw numbers to memorise. Fifty is halfway, whatever Mac you're on. Move them together, or one at a time.

Curves do the thinking

Pick Quiet, Balanced or Turbo and forget about it — or write your own °C:% points. It checks the temperature every couple of seconds and adjusts.

It won't let things cook

If your Mac gets genuinely hot while you're overriding the fans, fanknob gives them back to the firmware at 95 °C. You can move that line, or switch it off if you'd rather.

There's a CLI too

If you live in a terminal: fanknob set 40, fanknob preset quiet, or fanknob tui for a live dashboard. No sudo every time.

One thing worth knowing. If you pin the fans to a fixed speed, you're the one managing heat now, not your Mac. The 95 °C limit will catch you, but a curve is the better bet if you're leaving something running — it reacts, a fixed speed just sits there. Switch back to auto when you're done, or set a hold and let it expire by itself. (MacBook Airs don't have fans, so there's nothing to do there.)

Help

Troubleshooting

It says "helper not installed", or the light is orange
The background helper isn't running, so fanknob can see your fans but can't move them. Reinstalling usually sorts it. To start it yourself: 
sudo launchctl bootstrap system /Library/LaunchDaemons/com.fanknob.daemon.plist
My fans are stuck at one speed
Give them back to the firmware: 
fanknob auto
Curves and presets aren't doing anything
They need the helper running — it's the bit that keeps checking the temperature. Same fix as above.
Can I install it twice?
Best not to. Pick either the installer (or Homebrew) or a build from source — two helpers would end up fighting over the same fans, so the second one just refuses to start. brew uninstall --cask fanknob clears the first.
Still stuck?
The helper writes to /var/log/fanknobd.log, and fanknob status will tell you what it thinks is happening. If that's no help, open an issue and I'll take a look.
